=== VERDICT ===
Read the DISCARDED-field lists above. A situational term is viable for a sport only if
that sport has real fields with a real fill rate high enough to matter -- a field present
but null on nearly every play cannot carry a bonus. This probe deliberately stops here:
it reports what real fields exist, and does NOT propose a scoring formula. Designing
bonuses against unverified fields is the exact mistake the rest of these probes exist to
prevent, and any real change to dramaScoreLive() would also need a backfill plan, since
it would alter every historical drama_peak the app already displays.
